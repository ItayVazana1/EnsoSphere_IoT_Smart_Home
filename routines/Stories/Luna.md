# Luna – Seasonal Routine Story

## 🍂 Autumn
Luna, the beloved family dog, starts her day calmly in the living room, waiting patiently for someone to wake up. Around 07:30, she heads out for a walk with David, enjoying the cool breeze and peaceful streets. After returning home, she rests in the living room and later moves to the kitchen at 10:30 for breakfast.

She spends the quiet midday hours napping in the parents’ room. By 15:30, she perks up again for her favorite moment—an afternoon walk with Gavriella. After stretching her legs and sniffing around the neighborhood, she returns home to rest before dinner, which she enjoys at 19:30 in the kitchen. The rest of the evening is spent near the family in the living room, and late at night, she happily greets Kobe when he returns from work before heading to sleep.

## ❄️ Winter
Winter slows Luna down. Mornings are still anchored with her walk at 07:30, though she prefers shorter outings. She eats her breakfast at 10:30 and spends most of the day snuggled in the parents’ room, where it’s warm and quiet.

Afternoon walks are shorter—often just a balcony break with Gavriella. As always, she gets her dinner at 19:30. Evenings are cozy in the living room near the family. She always hears Kobe come in, and she greets him at the door with a wag before returning to her warm corner.

## 🌸 Spring
Spring brings out Luna’s playful side. After relaxing in the living room early in the morning, she heads out at 07:30 for a walk with David. She returns happy and hungry, eating breakfast at 10:30.

She lounges in the parents’ room until Gavriella returns, and by 15:30, she’s out again for a fun walk around the neighborhood. The day winds down with a satisfying dinner at 19:30 and time spent with the family in the living room. At night, she never misses greeting Kobe at the door before bedtime.

## ☀️ Summer
In summer, Luna adjusts to the heat by keeping cool indoors through the morning. Her walk with David still happens at 07:30, but it’s followed by long periods of rest in shaded spots around the house. At 10:30, she heads to the kitchen for her breakfast.

After a lazy midday, she goes out with Gavriella around 15:30—often for longer walks and sometimes even a fun detour. Dinner time is always at 19:30, and she enjoys the activity of a full kitchen. Her night ends cuddled up in the living room or by the door, awaiting Kobe’s return for one last tail-wag.
