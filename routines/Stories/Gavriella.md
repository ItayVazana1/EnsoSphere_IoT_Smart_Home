# Gavriella – Seasonal Routine Story

## 🍂 Autumn
Gavriella, the youngest in the family at age 10, starts her day in her cozy room before heading to the bathroom to get ready. By 07:30, she's in the kitchen having a quick breakfast. School occupies most of her morning and early afternoon, keeping her outside until 14:00. Once home, she unwinds in her room before taking Luna for their beloved afternoon walk.

After the walk, Gavriella refreshes herself and helps around the house—especially tidying up the living room. Later, she enjoys time in the kitchen either helping or simply being present with her family. At 19:30, she joins everyone for dinner. Her evenings wind down in the living room, often filled with light entertainment or bonding time before heading off to bed.

## ❄️ Winter
Despite the cold, Gavriella maintains her disciplined routine. She wakes up in her room and freshens up before breakfast in the kitchen. After heading to school, she returns by mid-afternoon and transitions indoors for most of the day. Luna’s winter walks are shorter but still happen—usually a brisk outing after school.

She helps around the house, particularly with cleaning the kids’ room and folding laundry. She spends a good chunk of her evening enjoying the warm atmosphere of the living room and kitchen. Dinner is still a family affair, followed by a calm evening before bed.

## 🌸 Spring
With spring in the air, Gavriella is noticeably more energetic. After her morning prep and breakfast, she heads to school. When she returns home, she quickly switches into home mode—dropping her backpack in her room and heading out for a fun walk with Luna through the neighborhood.

Post-walk, she plays or draws in her room. By the evening, she’s in and out of the living room and kitchen, helping prepare dinner or simply chatting with her parents. The day ends with a cozy evening in the living room before heading to bed.

## ☀️ Summer
Summer mornings are easier and slower. Without school, Gavriella gets to wake up at her own pace. After breakfast, she either spends time in her room or on the balcony enjoying the warmth. Midday activities include helping in the kitchen or living room, playing indoors, or getting some screen time.

Afternoons are dedicated to Luna, with longer walks and bonding. She freshens up, plays a bit, and helps set the table in the evening. Dinner is at 19:30 with the whole family, followed by a relaxed night in the living room and bedtime shortly after.
