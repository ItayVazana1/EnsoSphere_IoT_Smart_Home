# David – Seasonal Routine Story

## 🍂 Autumn
David begins his day early, rising around 06:00 and stepping outside shortly after for a refreshing morning walk with Luna. The fresh autumn air sets the tone for a productive day. After returning, he spends some quiet time on the balcony before heading out again—either for errands or work. By mid-morning, he's already in the car or outside, engaged in his responsibilities.

The afternoon finds him shifting between various areas: relaxing briefly, handling things around the house, or joining short family interactions. By early evening, he's often in the kitchen helping or preparing to settle in. At 19:30, he joins the entire family in the kitchen for dinner. Later, David enjoys quality time in the living room before returning to the bedroom for the night.

## ❄️ Winter
Winter brings a slower, warmer rhythm to David’s mornings. He starts the day in the parents’ room, easing into the day before getting ready. Even in the cold, he takes Luna out for her morning walk, making sure she stays active. After returning home, he usually remains indoors—spending more time in the balcony or kitchen as the weather outside remains harsh.

In the evenings, he is a consistent presence in the home. He shares meals, interacts with the kids, and especially cherishes quiet living room time with Mishel. David is a grounding presence in the house—steadfast and reliable, regardless of the season.

## 🌸 Spring
Spring energizes David. His day begins outdoors with Luna, often enjoying the awakening neighborhood during their early stroll. Afterward, he rotates between the balcony and brief trips in and out of the house. His spring routine feels more fluid—light, but still anchored in helpful participation.

In the evenings, he shares the space with the family, especially during dinner. His presence in the living room reflects the seasonal calm and family warmth. At night, he wraps things up early, retreating to the bedroom to rest and prepare for the next day.

## ☀️ Summer
David welcomes summer by starting his day outside or on the balcony. Mornings are warm and bright, and he adapts easily—taking Luna out for a longer morning walk before the heat peaks. He alternates between outdoor duties and indoor cool-down time.

In the afternoon, David can be found in the kitchen or checking in on the family. The highlight remains the family dinner at 19:30, where he connects with everyone. As night falls, he winds down in the living room and heads to the bedroom, closing the day in the same calm rhythm he began it with.
