# Mishel – Seasonal Routine Story

## 🍂 Autumn
Mishel starts her day around 06:30, after David, beginning with a short bathroom routine. She moves to the kitchen to prepare breakfast and lunches for the kids. By 08:00, she’s back in the parents' room getting dressed and ready to leave for work. She is out of the house by 08:30 and returns around 16:00.

Once home, she takes a short break in the kitchen before diving into house chores. She heads out again in the early evening for her gym session. After a quick shower, she cooks dinner and joins the family at 19:30 for their shared meal. Her evening ends with some relaxing time in the living room with David, before heading to bed at 22:30.

## ❄️ Winter
Winter grants Mishel a break—she stays home for the day. The morning is slow-paced, beginning with her usual bathroom routine and breakfast. She spends the majority of her time tidying up the house, organizing the kitchen, and folding laundry. Occasionally, she watches TV in the living room as she moves between tasks.

With no gym or work outside, her entire day is centered at home. She joins the rest of the family for dinner at 19:30 and spends the evening unwinding with David in the living room before heading to bed.

## 🌸 Spring
Spring feels like a return to her autumn rhythm. Mishel prepares breakfast, gets ready, and leaves for work by 08:30. After returning at 16:00, she dives into cleaning and cooking, then heads out for her regular gym session. She showers and returns to the kitchen in time for dinner with the family at 19:30.

In the evening, instead of staying in, she and David go out together around 21:00—perhaps for a walk, a café, or just fresh air. They return home to settle in before bed, ending the day feeling connected and recharged.

## ☀️ Summer
Summer changes the pace. Mishel returns home earlier from work—around 14:00—and uses that time for a refreshing afternoon nap. She then resumes her role in the kitchen, preparing meals and cleaning up.

In the evening, she dresses up and heads out to meet friends for a fun night out. After returning, she enjoys a short calm moment with David or the kids before heading to bed. Summer is her time to breathe, socialize, and reset.
