# Kobe – Seasonal Routine Story

## 🍂 Autumn
Kobe, a 16-year-old teenager with a part-time job at a local pizzeria, begins his mornings in his room before freshening up in the bathroom and grabbing breakfast in the kitchen. He heads to school and returns around 14:00, where he briefly snacks and relaxes in the kitchen.

Afternoons are filled with screen time, rest, and some light house interactions. As the evening nears, he prepares for work—showering and changing before leaving around 18:30. He works late, only returning home around 01:00. Once back, he grabs a quick bite in the kitchen before retreating to his room for a well-earned sleep.

## ❄️ Winter
In the winter, Kobe’s routine remains structured but leans more indoors due to the cold. Mornings still involve the same school prep sequence, but after returning home, he prefers resting in his room. He keeps warm, watches content, and occasionally helps out lightly in the kitchen.

Evenings follow the same work pattern—he leaves for the pizzeria after prepping, and the cold walk to work becomes part of his rhythm. After returning at night, he quietly navigates the dark kitchen for a snack before ending his day.

## 🌸 Spring
Spring brings renewed energy. Kobe begins his day with his usual school routine and returns home to rest and recharge. After school, he may help with small tasks or interact briefly with Gavriella or his parents before preparing for work.

Evenings feel lighter—his walk to the pizzeria is more pleasant, and he sometimes stays a bit longer to socialize. When he returns past midnight, he snacks and winds down with some music or phone time before sleeping.

## ☀️ Summer
Summer shifts Kobe’s rhythm. With school out, mornings are slower, though he still attends summer classes or learning sessions. Afterward, he spends time at home—either playing, watching content, or simply relaxing.

Afternoons are calm, and by early evening, he starts getting ready for another shift at the pizzeria. His night is long and consistent—working until late and returning quietly. He continues his pattern of snacking at night before ending his day in solitude and silence.
